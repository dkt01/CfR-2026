## Where the three polygon regions come from

`hoops`, `narrow_region` and `wide_open_region` are named stops in
`jetson/README.md`'s course tour, but `generate_obstacle_course.py` never
draws a rectangle for them. Their boundaries are derived from the hay bale
footprints in the SDF by `scripts/derive_polygons.py` (hand-painted
`region_guides.json` only picks the region and closes open ends) and stored in
`region_polygons.json` (world meters). `wide_open_region` is the
same "wide area" that `obstacle_randomizer_node`'s gap-bale wall separates
from the bucket section (see `jetson/README.md`'s "bucket section's entrance"
note, and the `generate_obstacle_course.py` `GAP_BALES`/`GAP_WALL_BOUNDS`
constants). The derived `wide_open_region` and `narrow_region` outlines meet
along the east wall, so a point within a few centimeters of it may match both.

## Display boxes vs. actual matching

`helical_ramp` and the three polygon regions are not boxes, so the bounds
shown in the table are display numbers only; `near`/`classify` test the real
shape:

- `helical_ramp` is really an annulus (inner/outer radius) swept through
  270 degrees; `in_helix()` checks the true radius range and angle sweep,
  which correctly excludes the hollow centre and the 90 degree wedge the
  tunnel sits in.
- `hoops`, `narrow_region` and `wide_open_region` are checked with
  point-in-polygon against `region_polygons.json`.
